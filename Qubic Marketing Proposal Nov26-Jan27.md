## **Introduction**

This proposal requests \$23,529 to fund the Qubic Marketing Team for November 2026 to January 2027, a reduction of \$3,186 (11.9%) from the previous term.

The team continues the corporate content style and organic-first approach introduced in the August–October term. Paid media remains removed from the budget. Reach and visibility are sustained through organic channels, SEO, video content and cross-department programming.

Marketing at Qubic operates across all departments rather than in isolation. The team works alongside the Science, Community and Core Tech teams so that Qubic is presented to every audience as one unified network. The target audience covers all ecosystem participants, not only investors.

This term introduces three team changes:

* **Hagrid** leads the team as Head of Marketing & Growth, overseeing all marketing activity, coordination and social media management.  
* **Nova** works on content management and light web maintenance, at 50% dedication.  
* **Nitzz** covers both video production and graphic design at 100% dedication.

Mr. Rose continues solely as treasurer, managing the funds and overseeing that deliverables are met. He does not hold a marketing role.
